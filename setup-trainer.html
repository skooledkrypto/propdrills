How to use this deck

Backtests are blunt about it: a candlestick pattern on its own is a weak edge. What turns it into a real setup is location + context + exits. So every pattern here is taught the same way — it only counts when it forms at a level you marked in advance, in a market whose structure agrees.

Work one pattern at a time. Read it here, then switch to Drill and set the focus to that single pattern until your accuracy is consistently high. Add the next one only when the first is automatic. The "No Setup" reps matter as much as the others — most of the tape is nothing, and learning to pass is a real skill.

Why drilling this works

Recognition is a trainable skill built by reps + immediate feedback. Live, your 3–9 PM window gives you maybe 1–3 setups a night. Here you can see hundreds. When your focused accuracy is high and stable, move to bar-replay on a real platform (NinjaTrader Market Replay or TradingView Bar Replay) to add live noise — then, and only then, buy the prop challenge.
