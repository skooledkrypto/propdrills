# PropDrills — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-17._

<!-- Keep in sync with src/core/legal.ts (the copy bundled into the apps). -->

- **Your training data stays on your device.** Drill progress, challenge history, settings, and
  your optional display name are stored locally (browser IndexedDB/localStorage on web,
  AsyncStorage on mobile). PropDrills has no email or password accounts, no analytics SDKs, no
  advertising, and no tracking. The app does not collect, sell, or share personal data.
- **Purchases.** If you buy PropDrills Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple, Google, or
  Stripe, and subscription status is managed by RevenueCat using a random anonymous app user
  ID. We never see your name, email, or payment details. RevenueCat processes purchase history
  and the anonymous ID to deliver your entitlement; see
  [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) for details.
- **Cloud Sync (optional).** If you turn on Cloud Sync, your progress backup (drill progress,
  challenge history, settings, optional display name, and the anonymous purchase ID) is stored
  on our servers keyed to a random sync code that only you hold — no email, no password, and
  nothing that identifies you personally. The cloud copy exists solely so your own devices can
  share progress and purchases; it is never used for anything else. "Delete cloud account" in
  Settings erases it immediately and permanently. If you never turn on Cloud Sync, nothing is
  uploaded.
- **Backups.** Progress backups you export are plain files under your control; outside of
  optional Cloud Sync, the app never uploads them anywhere.
- **Notifications.** Daily practice reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No
  push-notification service is used and nothing leaves your phone.
- **Data deletion.** On-device data is in your hands: use "Reset all progress & stats" in
  Settings, or uninstall the app. If you used Cloud Sync, "Delete cloud account" in Settings
  erases the cloud copy. For purchase records, manage your account with Apple or Google
  directly.
- **Children.** PropDrills is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
  data from anyone (it collects no personal data at all).
- **Changes.** If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above changes.
- **Contact.** Privacy questions: propdrills@gmail.com
