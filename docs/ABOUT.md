# About PropDrills

**Veteran built. Veteran owned. Veteran operated.**

PropDrills didn't start as a product. I built it for myself — a place to put in real repetitions reading MNQ price action, because watching charts wasn't making me better and paying for evaluation attempts is an expensive way to practice.

So I built the trainer I wanted: real historical MNQ sessions, drills that make you call location and pattern before the reveal, and a simulated prop-firm style evaluation with the same rules that end real accounts — trailing max drawdown, daily loss limits, a profit target, minimum trading days. Somewhere to make my mistakes on replayed data instead of live capital.

Once it existed, keeping it to myself felt like a waste.

## Why it's cheap

Most tools in this space are priced as if everyone passes their evaluation. Most attempts fail — and practicing shouldn't cost as much as the attempt itself. PropDrills is free to train, and Pro is priced so that money is never the reason someone doesn't get their reps in.

## What PropDrills is not

It's a practice tool — not a broker, not a prop firm, and not a signal service. Nothing here predicts the market or promises trading results. Real setups fail often, and trading futures is leveraged and risky. What PropDrills gives you is repetitions and honest feedback on your read — the rest is on you.
